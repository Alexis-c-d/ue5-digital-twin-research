# UE5 Chaos Physics as a Digital Twin Environment

Research project (in progress) evaluating **Unreal Engine 5 Chaos Physics** as a digital twin simulation environment for differential-drive mobile robots.

## Question
How close is the physical fidelity of UE5 to theoretical kinematic models, and what does that imply for the Sim2Real gap?

## Approach
- Compare simulated behavior against theoretical kinematic models
- Comparative analysis with Gazebo and Webots
- Literature review and structured scientific article
